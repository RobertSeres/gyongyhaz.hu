## 2026-03-06 - Structural Landmark Pattern
**Learning:** Keyboard users and screen reader users need efficient ways to bypass navigation/headers and jump to the main content (WCAG 2.4.1). Pairing a 'Skip to Content' (Hungarian: 'Ugrás a tartalomra') link with a sibling `<main>` landmark with `tabindex="-1"` and `focus:outline-none` satisfies this requirement cleanly without persistent visual focus rings. Crucially, site-wide landmarks like `<main>` and `<footer>` must be sibling elements; nesting `<footer>` within `<main>` is a semantic accessibility error.
**Action:** Always implement a skip-to-content link as the first child of the `<body>` element, and wrap the primary content area in a `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1" class="focus:outline-none">` landmark as a sibling of `<footer>`.
