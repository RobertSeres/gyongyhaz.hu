## 2026-03-05 - Structural Landmark Pattern
**Learning:** Single-page applications benefit immensely from a "Skip to Content" link paired with a focused `<main>` landmark element (`tabindex="-1"`) to allow keyboard and screen-reader users to bypass repetitive header navigation safely and cleanly.
**Action:** Always wrap primary content in `<main>` with `tabindex="-1"` and place an accessible "Skip to Content" link as the first body child.
