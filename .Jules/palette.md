## 2025-05-10 - Gallery Showcase Images WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1 Non-Text Content
**Learning:** Gallery showcase images without alt attributes cause screen readers to announce unhelpful asset URLs or skip visual context entirely for visually impaired users.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive Hungarian alt attributes matching the salon domain (fodrászat, műköröm, kozmetika, masszázs) for all gallery images.
