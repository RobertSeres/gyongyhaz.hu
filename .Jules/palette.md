## 2026-01-07 - Structural Landmark Pattern & Bypass Blocks
**Learning:** In a single-page marketing landing page with fixed navigation and heavy header content, keyboard and screen reader users must navigate through numerous interactive header links before reaching the core services and pricing. Implementing a 'Skip to Content' link targeting a `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1" class="focus:outline-none">` landmark satisfies WCAG 2.1 SC 2.4.1 (Bypass Blocks) and allows programmatic focus shift without persistent focus outlines. High z-index (`z-[150]`) and fixed positioning ensure it stays visible over fixed navigation bars when focused.
**Action:** Pair skip-to-content links with a `<main>` landmark with `tabindex="-1"` and `focus:outline-none`, ensuring high z-index and high contrast against background themes.
