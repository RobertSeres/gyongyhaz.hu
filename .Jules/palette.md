## 2026-01-08 - Structural Landmark Pattern & Skip Link Accessibility
**Learning:** Monolithic landing pages with sticky headers and many navigation links can be frustrating for keyboard and screen reader users without landmark navigation and bypass mechanisms. Implementing a 'Skip to Content' link paired with `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1" class="focus:outline-none">` satisfies WCAG 2.1 SC 2.4.1 (Bypass Blocks) and allows instantaneous navigation past repetitive navigation blocks without leaving an unsightly focus outline when focused programmatically.
**Action:** Always ensure landing pages have a visible-on-focus skip link as the very first focusable child of `<body>` with high contrast against the background (`bg-pastel-purple text-dark-bg`) and high `z-index` (`z-[150]`), and structure content with semantic `<main>` and `<footer>` sibling landmarks.
