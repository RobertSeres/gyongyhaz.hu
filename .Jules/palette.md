## 2026-08-06 - Structural Landmark Pattern
**Learning:** For monolithic single-page websites, keyboard and screen reader users frequently face navigation roadblocks because they must tab through dense header menus to reach the primary content. Implementing a high-contrast 'Skip to Content' (Hungarian: 'Ugrás a tartalomra') link paired with a semantic `<main>` landmark with `tabindex="-1"` and `focus:outline-none` satisfies WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 2.4.1 (Bypass Blocks). Placing this link as the absolute first child of the `<body>` element ensures it is immediately focusable, while making the `<main>` element a sibling of `<footer>` keeps document landmarks clean and screen-reader compliant.
**Action:** When working on monolithic or landing-page sites, prioritize this pattern to enable instant bypassing of navigation blocks, always ensuring Hungarian localization and proper sibling structure for structural landmarks.
