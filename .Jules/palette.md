## 2026-07-26 - [Skip to Content & Semantic Landmarks]
**Learning:** Monolithic landing pages often contain numerous interactive elements within header navigation and external widgets that keyboard or assistive technology users must tab through. Implementing the 'Structural Landmark Pattern'—pairing a prominent, localized skip link with a `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1">` container—greatly enhances keyboard bypass capacity, satisfying WCAG 2.1 Bypass Blocks guidelines (SC 2.4.1). Removing focus outline from `<main>` via `focus:outline-none` when programmatically focused avoids undesirable visual rings while ensuring assistive tools properly shift context.
**Action:** When working with long-scroll or monolithic landing pages, immediately audit for proper HTML5 landmark elements (`<main>`, `<header>`, `<footer>`) and place a localized, visually hidden but focusable skip link as the very first child of `<body>` to maximize navigation convenience.
