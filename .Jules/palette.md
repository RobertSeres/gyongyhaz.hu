## 2026-03-30 - Descriptive Alt Text on Portfolio/Gallery Images
**Learning:** Gallery grid images without `alt` text leave screen reader users completely unaware of the visual services offered (e.g. hair styling, nail care, cosmetics, massage) by a salon. Providing concise Hungarian `alt` text matching the site language restores context and complies with WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1.
**Action:** Always audit `<img>` elements in landing page portfolios and ensure localized, descriptive `alt` attributes are present.
