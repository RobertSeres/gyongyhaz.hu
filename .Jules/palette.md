## 2026-01-15 - Accessible Modal Close Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only close buttons (using `&times;` or FontAwesome icon `fa-times`) in modal components lack accessible names unless explicitly labeled with `aria-label`.
**Action:** Always include an `aria-label` in the site's primary language (e.g., `aria-label="Bezárás"`) on close buttons lacking visible text labels.
