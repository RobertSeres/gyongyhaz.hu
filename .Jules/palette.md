## 2026-08-11 - [Structural Landmark Pattern for Monolithic Layouts]
**Learning:** In a large, monolithic landing page (e.g., Gyöngy Ház), keyboard and screen reader accessibility suffers from structural bypass issues (WCAG 2.4.1). Introducing a high-contrast 'Skip to Content' link immediately inside the `<body>` paired with a target `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1" class="focus:outline-none">` offers a seamless keyboard navigation bypass around heavy navigation and hero sections without introducing visual layout clutter or unintended focus rings.
**Action:** Always verify that skip links are placed as the first interactive descendant of `<body>` and position them with high visual prominence (`focus:fixed`, `focus:top-4`, `focus:left-4`, high `z-index`) and verify correct focus shift using automated Playwright scripts.
