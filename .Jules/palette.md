## 2025-05-18 - Structural Landmark Pattern & Skip Navigation
**Learning:** In single-page applications with fixed navigation bars and complex interactive overlays, screen reader and keyboard users face significant friction navigating repetitive headers without a direct bypass mechanism. Adding a "Skip to content" link paired with a `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1" class="focus:outline-none">` landmark satisfies WCAG 2.4.1 (Bypass Blocks) and guarantees that programmatically shifted focus doesn't produce an unwanted persistent outline while remaining fully accessible.
**Action:** Always place the skip-to-content anchor as the first child of `<body>` with high z-index fixed focus styling (`z-[150]`), and pair it with `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1">` wrapping the primary content between navigation and footer.
